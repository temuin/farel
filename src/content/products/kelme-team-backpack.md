A team backpack sized for training and travel days. A separate ventilated
compartment keeps boots away from clean kit, and the padded back panel carries
comfortably on longer journeys.

- Approximately 25 L main compartment
- Ventilated lower boot compartment
- Padded laptop sleeve and side bottle pockets
- Water-resistant base panel

Available for crest embroidery and squad-name application.
