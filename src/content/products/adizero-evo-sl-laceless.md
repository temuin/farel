**Adidas Adizero Evo SL Laceless: Full Description**

The Adizero Evo SL has become a favorite among runners as a light, fast everyday trainer. The Laceless version keeps what made it popular and removes the laces. Slip your foot in and you’re ready to go, with no tying, no loosening and no loose ends.

**Fit and upper**
Instead of a traditional lacing system, the shoe uses a stretchy, sock-like knit tongue and an engineered mesh upper with elastic construction. Together they wrap the midfoot snugly and move with your foot. The upper is breathable and has no eyestays, which gives the shoe a clean, streamlined look. Supportive paneling adds structure where the foot needs it.

**Cushioning**
The midsole is Adidas LightstrikePro, the same super-light foam that comes from its race-day technology. It gives a springy, responsive ride with good energy return, so the shoe works for easy miles, tempo sessions and faster days.

**Grip and durability**
A rubber outsole gives reliable traction on road surfaces in dry or wet conditions and holds up to regular training.

**Design**
The silhouette is minimal and sleek, with taped 3-Stripes along the side. Some colorways also have reflective 3-Stripes for visibility in low light. The look is sporty but clean enough to wear casually, so the shoe works for running and for everyday outfits.

**Key features**

* Laceless slip-on design with a stretch-knit tongue
* Breathable, foot-hugging mesh upper
* LightstrikePro midsole for light, responsive cushioning
* Rubber outsole for road grip
* Textile sockliner
* Taped 3-Stripes branding
* Colorways include Core Black, Cloud White and Off White
