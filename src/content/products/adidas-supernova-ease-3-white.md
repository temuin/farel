**adidas Supernova Ease: Ease into Your Comfy Runs**

The adidas Supernova Ease is made for your most comfortable runs. A full-length Dreamstrike hyper-foam midsole delivers lighter, plush cushioning with a dynamic ride, while the soft Comfort Heel Fit keeps your heel secure and cushioned with every step. The semi-blown rubber outsole gives a smooth, steady feel, supporting each stride and transition.

Designed as a stable daily running shoe, it works well for short to mid-distance runs. Whether you’re building your running routine or training for your first race, it offers comfort and support without added weight. Compared with models like the Supernova Stride, which focuses on heel-to-toe transitions, the Supernova Ease puts the emphasis on an easy, cushioned feel for relaxed runs.
