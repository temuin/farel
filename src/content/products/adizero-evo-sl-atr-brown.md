**adidas Adizero Evo SL ATR: Evo SL Speed for Every Terrain and Season**

The adidas Adizero Evo SL ATR is the all-terrain evolution of the Evo SL, the lightweight trainer that became one of the standout running shoes of 2025. ATR stands for All-Terrain Running, and the shoe is built to stay fast and stable when conditions turn wet, muddy, or uneven, without losing the lightness and sleek look that made the original so popular.

Cushioning comes from the same full-length Lightstrike Pro midsole as the road version, giving a smooth, responsive, propulsive ride that works well at faster paces. The outsole is the main upgrade. Continental rubber with 1.5mm lugs gives reliable bite on damp pavement, gravel, park paths, and light off-road detours, and the heel lockdown has been improved for a more secure feel when the surface gets less predictable.

The upper has been reworked for tougher conditions. It uses an abrasion-resistant ripstop material, with a wraparound mudguard and a reinforced toebox to protect against scuffs and debris. Water-repellent protection helps keep you comfortable on wet days, and reflective details improve visibility in low light.

Fast on roads and confident on mixed terrain, it suits runners who want one lightweight trainer for weekly runs, tempo sessions, and workouts through every season.
