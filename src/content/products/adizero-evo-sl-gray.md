**adidas Adizero Evo SL: Race-Day Speed for Everyday Training**

The adidas Adizero Evo SL takes the DNA of the record-breaking Adizero Adios Pro Evo 1 and brings it to your daily runs. It is a lightweight, fast-feeling trainer for runners who want a taste of race-day performance without needing a bib.

A full-length Lightstrike Pro midsole gives a smooth, dynamic ride with strong energy return, without a stiff plate. That makes it a good fit for tempo days, progression runs, or any session where you want to push the pace. The engineered mesh upper is lightweight and breathable, with a sock-like fit that keeps you cool and secure. A Continental rubber outsole provides reliable traction and durability on the road.

At about 224g for men and 188g for women, it is the lightest training shoe in the adidas running lineup. It suits beginners looking for their first serious running shoe, experienced marathoners wanting a versatile trainer, and everyone in between.
